Tutorial in english:

Installation tutorial: Requirements: a USB drive, the Rufus program, Secure Boot disabled on your PC, and the OS .iso file.
Disabling Secure Boot: To do this, enter your PC's BIOS settings (on most machines, this is done by pressing the DELETE key, though it depends on the motherboard brand). Navigate to the BIOS "Boot" section, locate "Secure Boot," set it to "Disabled," and that's it—simply restart your PC and boot back into Windows.

Part 2: Bootable USB: Open Rufus to create the bootable USB drive. Once open, look for the "SELECT" button, click it, and choose the AB-OS .iso file. Then, wait for Rufus to copy the .iso to the USB drive (Note: Rufus will erase *everything* currently on the USB drive to make it bootable, so back up your files to a separate folder first). Once the copying is complete, restart your PC.

Part 3: Starting AB-OS: Once you have the bootable USB drive ready, plug it into your PC and access the boot menu (for MSI, press F11 repeatedly; for Gigabyte, press F12). Turn on the PC, boot from the USB drive, and you're all set. Congratulations, you now have Absolute Solver OS! :D (Note: If you need help with commands, simply type "HELP" in the terminal to see the full list; for any compatibility issues, please email leonal.osuna13@gmail.com)
